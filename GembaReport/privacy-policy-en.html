Privacy Policy

This is the privacy policy for SpotReport (Japanese name: その場レポート, the "App"). Please read it before using the App.

Information we collect

The developer does not collect any personal information (name, address, email, etc.) from you.
Everything you enter into the App, including photos, is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer's servers.
The App shows no ads and uses no analytics or advertising identifiers (IDFA).

Data stored on your device

  • Projects (title, work date, location, person in charge, notes)
  • Customers you enter (name, company, address, phone, email)
  • Site photos (originals, edited versions and report-size copies)
  • Templates, including form PDFs and scanned pages you import
  • Reports (field values, signatures and generated PDFs)
  • Phrases, company profile (name, person in charge, address, contact details, logo) and app settings

Deleting the App removes this data from the device. If iCloud Backup is enabled, iOS may include it in your backup.

Camera and photo library access

The App asks for camera and photo library permission to capture and import site photos. Captured photos are stored in the App's own storage on the device and are saved to your photo library only if you enable that setting.
GPS location data is removed from report images. When sharing original photos you can choose whether to strip location data.

Sharing

Report PDFs and photos are shared through the standard iOS share sheet directly to the app or service you choose (Mail, LINE, AirDrop, etc.). The developer never receives or stores shared content. Handling by the receiving service is governed by that service's privacy policy.

Files received from other apps

Photos and PDFs shared into the App from other apps are stored on the device and are not transmitted anywhere.

In-app purchases

  • Pro (monthly / yearly) is an auto-renewing subscription. It renews automatically and is charged to your Apple ID unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Pro (lifetime) is a one-time, permanent unlock tied to the purchasing Apple ID.
  • Purchases can be re-applied with "Restore Purchases" using the same Apple ID.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings. For billing questions, contact App Store support.

Purchases are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

Third parties

The developer does not provide or sell your data to any third party.

Your responsibility

Photos and customer records you store in the App may contain personal information about your clients or their property. You are responsible for handling such information in accordance with applicable law and your agreements with your clients.

Minors

Minors should make in-app purchases only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Disclaimer

The developer is not liable for any damages arising from use of the App. Features may be changed, suspended or discontinued without notice.
