# Gunpla hobbyist pain points, unmet needs, and willingness to pay (overseas + Japan), as of Sept 2026

Method note: Search was done on 2026-09-23 with WebSearch. Nearly every WebFetch was blocked by the egress proxy (apps.apple.com, costumary.com, hjweb.jp, mygunplacollection.app, ko-fi.com, akibabrief.com), so most facts come from search-engine snippets and **have not been checked against the full page**. Search tools returned **no Reddit r/Gunpla threads at all**, so this file has **no verbatim Reddit quotes**. The "frequency/intensity" ranking below is therefore inferred from how many tools, services, and accounts exist for each need. It is not based on counted user complaints.

## Q1. Restock and availability pain (P-Bandai, Japanese-first restock news, scalping, proxies). How do fans learn about restocks, and are alert services paid?

### Takeaway
This is the most intense pain, and Japan has the most tooling for it. Japanese fans already have free push-alert apps (入荷Now, 入荷NAVI), many X alert accounts, and since May 2026 an official Bandai app whose feature lets fans vote on which kits get reissued. Overseas fans mostly rely on retailer pages and social media. That leaves a gap for an English "Japan restock → overseas availability" alert. It needs a reliable data pipeline (scraping Japanese shops and proxies), though, and the restock-alert niche in Japan shows no sign of paying users: the tools are free, ad- or affiliate-funded, or run on Patreon at about $1.

### Cited Findings
**Japan: the need is already served by free tools**
- X accounts dedicated to restock alerts exist, for example 「ガンプラ入荷速報【サボリーマン/入荷NAVI】」(@aanc20) and 「ガンプラ再販通知速報」(@Gundam_Alert) — [X @aanc20](https://x.com/aanc20), [X @Gundam_Alert](https://x.com/Gundam_Alert)
- The 入荷NAVI app sends push alerts when Gunpla is restocked or preorders reopen on Amazon, Rakuten, Surugaya, DMM and other shops. 入荷Now is a free app with push alerts and lets users choose which products and stores to follow (search snippet; not verified on the page) — [nyuka-now.com](https://nyuka-now.com/archives/6830)
- There are also restock-roundup and schedule sites: リーチャ (a Threads account plus a website) and spotgeeks, which has a calendar, search, and rarity ratings — [harmonizers-jp.com](https://harmonizers-jp.com/), [Threads @reachout4gunpla](https://www.threads.com/@reachout4gunpla?hl=ja), [spotgeeks](https://spotgeeks.news/archives/1308)
- A note article on setting up Amazon restock alerts exists, which shows the need is strong enough that people write how-to guides for it — [note: 遊びスイッチ](https://note.com/asobi_switch/n/nd57d54ed6f24)
- A blog explains how people who seem to "know the restock date" predict it — [トイコレクト](https://toycollect.blog/ganpurasaihanbi-wakaruhito/)
- **Official competitor:** BANDAI SPIRITS' official fan community app 「ビルダーズノート」 launched on 2026-05-12. Its three main features are 「ガンプラ再販会議」 (a reissue vote), 「ガンプラ製作ノート」 (build notes), and a community. The reissue meeting is held four times a year, a "selection election" to narrow down candidate kits runs every month, and voting tickets are earned through build logs and missions in the app. A 再販抽選会 (reissue lottery) for MG kits such as Marasai and Perfect Gundam has also been held — [PR TIMES (BANDAI SPIRITS)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000069.000028758.html), [電撃ホビーウェブ launch](https://hobby.dengeki.com/news/3004950/), [電撃ホビーウェブ lottery](https://hobby.dengeki.com/news/3038089/), [gn-app.com/reprint](https://www.gn-app.com/ja/reprint)

**Overseas**
- Premium Bandai does not ship outside Japan, and its FAQ says an account cannot even be registered with an overseas address. Bandai runs a separate Premium Bandai USA store (search snippet) — [akibabrief (snippet only; fetch blocked)](https://akibabrief.com/guide/p-bandai-tamashii-web-exclusives-overseas/)
- It is reported that P-Bandai has banned proxy purchases, including through ZenMarket, since July 2023, and Buyee says "proxy purchasing services will be unavailable for this site" (search snippet) — [MyFigureCollection blog](https://myfigurecollection.net/blogpost/60989), [Buyee p-bandai page](https://shop.buyee.jp/p-bandai?lang=en). **Unverified/contested:** a public GitHub repo researching proxy costs records that the evidence for the P-Bandai ban rests on a single third-party blog, and it later replaced its site-wide rule with per-proxy records — [GitHub muras3/poc_proxycost PR#440](https://github.com/muras3/poc_proxycost/pull/440), [PR#445](https://github.com/muras3/poc_proxycost/pull/445). Treat the claim that "all proxies are banned" as unconfirmed.
- For overseas fans, the advice is to follow hobby news sites and retailer social media for P-Bandai announcements. Authorized retailers such as P-Rex Hobby post P-Bandai restock alerts on Instagram and Facebook — [P-Rex Hobby](https://www.prexhobby.com/blogs/gundam-101/what-is-p-bandai-how-to-buy-premium-bandai-kits-in-north-america)
- Official English sources: the Bandai Hobby global launch schedule and @BandaiHobbyUS on X. Retailer restock pages: HobbySearch (1999.co.jp, English restock page), GundamExtra's restock schedules, USA Gundam Store, and Shokunin Gunpla — [Bandai Hobby schedule](https://global.bandai-hobby.net/en-us/schedule/), [@BandaiHobbyUS](https://twitter.com/bandaihobbyus?lang=en), [HobbySearch restock](https://www.1999.co.jp/eng/gundam/restock/), [GundamExtra](https://gundamextra.com/collections/restock-schedules), [USA Gundam Store](https://www.usagundamstore.com/collections/restock), [Shokunin](https://shokuningunpla.com/blogs/restocked-new-arrivals)
- HLJ has a support article titled "Why Wasn't My Gundam Order Filled?" that says per-person limits apply to Bandai Hobby items and that strict limits on popular items keep scalpers and resellers from taking over. This is evidence that allocation shortfalls are an ongoing pain for overseas buyers — [HLJ support](https://support.hlj.com/hc/en-us/articles/20580400091417-Why-Wasn-t-My-Gundam-Order-Filled)
- Counterpoint: Bandai keeps every mold and can reprint any kit, so a kit that is hard to find is usually reissued eventually if you wait. The article dates from 2021, the pandemic shortage period — [cmwolfe.net (2021)](https://www.cmwolfe.net/posts/2021/01/is-there-really-a-gunpla-shortage/)
- On TikTok, bots buying up P-Bandai restocks is a topic in its own right (only the search-page title was seen) — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/bots-take-everything-of-the-premium-bandai-restock)

**Existing English alert services and whether they are paid**
- Gunpla.Deals is run by an individual, uses the tagline "Build More. Spend Less.", makes money from affiliate codes, and offers **ad removal for $1/month on Patreon**. A Ko-fi page exists, and a search snippet mentions restock notifications — [gunpla.deals](https://gunpla.deals/), [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/gunpladeals)
- In the GUNPLA SA (South Africa) retailer Discord, members get notifications about low stock, restocks, and price drops (free; run by the retailer) — [gunplasa.com](https://gunplasa.com/pages/how-to-join-our-gunpla-sa-discord-community)
- Generic restock-alert tools also exist: the TYPA iOS app and a $50/month "pfpmonitors" monitor sold on Gumroad (the latter looks aimed at resellers; its Gunpla coverage is unconfirmed) — [TYPA](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6744125469), [Gumroad pfpmonitors](https://pfp.gumroad.com/l/pfpmonitors)
- top.gg has a "gunpla" tag for Discord bots, but no dedicated P-Bandai restock bot was found — [top.gg gunpla](https://top.gg/tag/gunpla)

### Inferences
- In Japan the restock-alert space is crowded: free apps, X accounts, and now Bandai's own app. A solo developer would struggle to compete there.
- Overseas there is room for an English app that says "this kit is getting a Japanese reissue next month; it will reach HLJ, HobbySearch, and US retailers around this time." The real value, though, is data collection: scraping Japanese sources and restock information from several retailers. Beating paid monitor bots on speed is hard for a solo developer.
- Among the services found, the only confirmed payment in the restock/deals space is **$1/month on Patreon for ad removal**, which suggests low willingness to pay. Scalper-oriented monitors ($50/month) are a separate market.

### Gaps
- No English restock-alert service with a confirmed Gunpla-specific paid tier was found.
- Reddit r/Gunpla posts about restock frustration could not be pulled (search returned none), so there are no quotes.
- gunjap.net and gundamkitscollection could not be confirmed as sources of restock information.
- The current state (2026) of P-Bandai's proxy ban is unconfirmed.

## Q2. Backlog management: how common is it, and how do people track it?

### Takeaway
A backlog (積みプラ, "pile of shame") is common enough that it has its own word in Japanese, and the market for tracking apps is **already crowded in both Japan and overseas**. Japan alone has at least four apps (Tsumina, PLADOCK, GUNPLAB, TSUMI TSUMI), overseas has several (My Gunpla Collection, Gunpla Pal, Gunpla Catalog, Collector, and PlamoTrack for self-hosting), and Bandai's official app now covers build logs too. Differentiating on backlog tracking alone will be hard.

### Cited Findings
- Tsumina (iOS/Android): tracks progress with four statuses (積み/製作中/製作完了/欲しい, i.e. backlog, in progress, done, wanted), registers photos, notes and tags, supports barcode scanning, and has stats. Yahoo! News expert articles have covered it. Snippets mention a paid membership, but the price is unconfirmed — [Yahoo!ニュース](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/expert/articles/d78ba42ed82b0b1826152a1bcf508547bd93bad5), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/tsumina-%E7%A9%8D%E3%81%BF%E3%83%97%E3%83%A9%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86%E3%82%A2%E3%83%97%E3%83%AA/id1524020362), [tsumina.app](https://tsumina.app/)
- PLADOCK: a backlog manager built around a "maintenance dock" concept that covers every kind of model kit. It advertises being free — [pladock.com](https://www.pladock.com/)
- GUNPLAB (ガンプラボ): tracks both kits and paint inventory — [gunplab.fun](https://gunplab.fun/)
- TSUMI TSUMI: no install, no sign-up, completely free, with barcode support — [tsumitsumi](https://tsumitsumi.vercel.app/tsumitsumi-lp.html)
- Apps introduced on YouTube include "Tsumina & Paint Manager" — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCaHZaNbnFE)
- Overseas: My Gunpla Collection (tracks from backlog to finished build, wishlist, shopping list; **local use is free, cross-device sync is premium**) — [mygunplacollection.app](https://mygunplacollection.app/)
- Gunpla Pal is described as "the closest thing to a purpose-built Gunpla collection tracker", but it is an individual developer's web app (Vercel, GitHub) — [Costumary comparison (snippet)](https://www.costumary.com/blog/gunpla-tracking-tools-2026), [GitHub](https://github.com/Evan-Roberts-808/Gunpla-Pal)
- PlamoTrack: a self-hosted tracker with a Kanban board and an MCP server for Claude/ChatGPT, with data exportable as CSV. It shows demand from power users who care about owning their data — [GitHub PlamoTrack](https://github.com/DeusMaximus/plamotrack)
- Others: Gunpla Catalog (iOS; Favorite / I have lists), Collector: Figure Tracker (covers several collectible categories; free with in-app purchases), Gundam Place (iOS) — [AppAdvice](https://appadvice.com/app/gunpla-catalog/1536081022), [Collector](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759530921), [Gundam Place](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gundam-place/id6479946774)
- YouTube series such as "Gunpla Backlog Buster" and Gunpla 101 articles on tackling a backlog suggest backlogs are common in overseas culture too — [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL44xZ-OMhqI77PJ7IWvjAia3vtwx26fLE), [Gunpla 101](https://www.gunpla101.com/how-to-confront-your-gunpla-backlog-during-quarantine/)
- A Facebook group post announcing a Gunpla collection tracking app was found, which suggests individual developers keep entering this space — [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/gunplatnt/posts/2636269066713999/)

### Inferences
- Needs are clearly "met": at least ten apps exist, and several are free. The existence of Tsumina's paid membership and My Gunpla Collection's premium sync suggests a freemium model with sync and backup as the paid feature is the standard. Revenue per app is probably small.
- Bandai's official 製作ノート records builds and is tied to reissue voting, which gives Japanese users a strong reason to log builds there. That is a threat to third-party apps in Japan.
- Differentiation ideas: English-speaking users, an accurate kit database (grade, release date, P-Bandai flag, reissue history), and tying the backlog to "is this kit being reissued, and where can I buy it".

### Gaps
- No quantitative data (surveys, Reddit polls) on backlog size or how many people track it in spreadsheets or Notion.
- Download counts, ratings and revenue for each app were not obtained (App Store pages were blocked).

## Q3. Build logs, paint recipes, tool inventory, and translating Japanese manuals

### Takeaway
Manual translation is a **weak pain**. Since around late 2016 Bandai manuals include Japanese and English, and there are also Bandai's manual site, scan sites, and Google Translate's camera mode. Paint recipes and inventory are **already covered by general miniature-painting apps** (paintRack, PaintVault and others). What looks unmet is an app specialised for Gunpla that ties together paint (Mr. Color etc.), masking, and build steps.

### Cited Findings
- Since late 2016, Bandai model kits, Gunpla included, have Japanese and English text on the box and manual. Google Translate's camera mode is recommended, and Dalong.net has scans of almost every retail kit (search snippet) — [Galactic Trove](https://galactictrove.com/blog-how-to-read-gunpla-manual-for-beginners/)
- Bandai has a new site for finding lost manuals — [Back2Gaming](https://www.back2gaming.com/gundam/where-to-find-gundam-instruction-manual/)
- English color guides and manuals are published on Mech9 — [mech9](https://www.mech9.com/p/gundam-color-guides-manuals.html)
- Paint management: paintRack (29,000+ paints, barcodes, recipes), Mini Paint Recipe Manager (label scanning, recipe cards), PaintVault (112+ brands including Mr. Hobby and Tamiya, explicitly lists Gunpla), HobbyTracker (paint codes, weathering materials, shopping list) — [paintRack](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paintrack/id1490523130), [Mini Paint Recipe Manager](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mini-paint-recipe-manager/id6747835376), [PaintVault](https://www.paint-vault.com/), [HobbyTracker](https://apps.apple.com/cl/app/hobbytracker/id6748728885)
- A comparison article says Gunpla Pal users doing custom paint work who want to manage Mr. Color inventory, masking plans, and build stages "may outgrow it" (snippet) — [Costumary](https://www.costumary.com/blog/gunpla-tracking-tools-2026)
- Bandai's official ビルダーズノート includes a 製作ノート (build log) feature and runs a 製作ノートコンテスト (build-log contest) — [電撃ホビーウェブ](https://hobby.dengeki.com/news/3004950/)

### Inferences
- A translation app is low priority because the problem is largely solved.
- A build log combined with paint recipes combined with kit data has room to differentiate, because it links build stages for a specific kit to paint recipes. Implementing it takes little hobby expertise. Paint databases (Mr. Color numbers etc.) can be sourced from public lists, but check licensing.

### Gaps
- No evidence that P-Bandai kits and older kits have Japanese-only manuals (likely, but unverified).
- No user voices (quotes) on the demand for build logs or paint recipes.

## Q4. Price tracking and collection value (eBay / Mercari resale)

### Takeaway
Tools already exist: general collectible valuation apps (Sparrow Collect, PriceCharting-style apps) and Apify scraper APIs that pull Japanese resale prices. A Gunpla-only "collection value" app is technically feasible if the data comes from Apify or similar. Most Gunpla fans build their kits, though, so demand for asset-value tracking is probably limited to collectors of P-Bandai items and kept unbuilt kits (inference).

### Cited Findings
- Apify's "Japan Figure & Gunpla Resale Value" API returns HLJ list price, Mandarake used prices, sold prices on Yahoo Auctions and Mercari, and the resale premium. A separate HLJ price and stock API also exists — [Apify resale](https://apify.com/jpmarketdata/japan-figure-gunpla-resale-value/api/openapi), [Apify HLJ](https://apify.com/jpmarketdata/hlj-hobby-market-checker/api/cli)
- Sparrow Collect: AI scanning and price estimates across 54 categories, pulling from eBay, Mercari, BrickLink and 45 marketplaces in total (iOS/Android) — [sparrowcollect.com](https://www.sparrowcollect.com/)
- A 2026 roundup compares seven free and paid "what is my stuff worth" apps — [Underpriced AI](https://underpricedai.com/blog/what-is-my-stuff-worth-apps)

### Inferences
- A solo developer could build price tracking on top of a third-party API such as Apify. Running costs and terms-of-service risk (scraping Mercari and Yahoo Auctions) need checking.
- This could be a paid feature for collectors, but no evidence of Gunpla-specific demand was found.

### Gaps
- No evidence of how much Gunpla fans want to track resale value (e.g. Reddit threads).
- Apify API pricing was not checked.

## Q5. Willingness to pay: paid Gunpla-related digital services, Patreon, paid Discords, paid apps

### Takeaway
Evidence of paying is **weak**. What was confirmed: Gunpla.Deals' **$1/month Patreon for ad removal**, a Patreon that gives supporters a Discord link (Gunpla Meli), Tsumina's paid membership (price unknown), and My Gunpla Collection's paid sync. No paid Discord for restock alerts or a high-priced subscription for Gunpla fans was found. The main Discords (Gunpla Builder's Association, Gundam HQ) are free.

### Cited Findings
- Gunpla.Deals: $1/month on Patreon to remove ads, plus affiliate income — [gunpla.deals](https://gunpla.deals/)
- Gunpla Meli: a Patreon post that gives patrons a Discord link (Patreon support for a creator) — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/discord-link-48688202)
- Free Discords: Gunpla Builder's Association (official Discord server), Gundam HQ, and many servers tagged gunpla on DISBOARD and Discord Me — [GBA](https://discord.com/servers/gunpla-builder-s-association-124916145705713664), [Gundam HQ](https://discord.com/invite/gundam-hq-363541761886519307), [DISBOARD](https://disboard.org/servers/tag/gunpla)
- Freemium apps: My Gunpla Collection (paid sync), Collector: Figure Tracker (free with in-app purchases), Tsumina (paid membership mentioned) — [My Gunpla Collection](https://mygunplacollection.app/), [Collector](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759530921), [Tsumina](https://tsumina.app/)
- Several free competitors: PLADOCK (free), TSUMI TSUMI (completely free), and Bandai's official ビルダーズノート (no evidence of payment found) — [PLADOCK](https://www.pladock.com/), [TSUMI TSUMI](https://tsumitsumi.vercel.app/tsumitsumi-lp.html)

### Inferences
- Adding the evidence up: Gunpla fans spend a lot of money on the kits themselves, but willingness to pay for digital tools is low (the benchmark is $1/month). The realistic model is freemium (a one-time purchase in the low thousands of yen, or a few hundred yen a month for sync, backup, and unlimited items) combined with affiliate income from retailer links such as HLJ, HobbySearch and Amazon. Affiliate revenue from Gunpla.Deals is the precedent.
- **Ranking of pain points (inferred from how much tooling exists and how intense the problem is; not based on counted complaints):**
  1. Restocks, availability and getting hold of P-Bandai kits: intensity high. Japan is well served by free tools; English is fragmented. Solo-dev feasibility: medium (the data pipeline is the hard part, and there is scraping ToS risk).
  2. Backlog and collection management: common, but already heavily served (ten or more apps plus the official app). Feasibility: high. Differentiation: low.
  3. Linking build logs, paint recipes and tools: moderately served (general miniature apps). A Gunpla-specific combination has room. Feasibility: high, with little hobby knowledge needed.
  4. Price and collection value: served by existing general apps and APIs. Demand is limited. Feasibility: medium (depends on third-party API cost).
  5. Manual translation: largely solved (English on manuals since 2016, scan sites, Google Lens). Low priority.
- The hobby-expertise barrier for a solo developer: a kit database (hundreds to thousands of kits with grade, release date and P-Bandai flag) is essential, and building it takes work. Community sites such as GunplaDB and Dalong.net exist, but reusing their data needs licensing checks.

### Gaps
- No revenue, download numbers or conversion rates were found for any app.
- No quantitative survey of willingness to pay (e.g. a Reddit poll) was found.
- Whether a paid restock Discord exists, including private ones, could not be confirmed. Private invite-only servers are hard to find by search.
