## 1. Bandai Namco IR: Gundam IP / Hobby sales, overseas ratio, shipments, factories

### Takeaway
Gundam IP sales rose from ¥153.5B (FY3/2025) to a record ¥254.3B (FY3/2026, +65.6%). Q1 of FY3/2027 came in around ¥65.6B, the fifth quarter in a row above ¥60B. Management says ¥300B is "in sight", driven by Europe and North America. Gunpla has been roughly 50% overseas by revenue since about 2020. Bandai is still adding factory capacity because demand keeps outrunning supply.

### Cited Findings
- FY3/2026 Gundam IP sales were ¥254.3B, up 65.6% YoY and a record. Drivers were GQuuuuuuX, the Osaka Expo "GUNDAM NEXT FUTURE PAVILION", and Gunpla, which was strong all year. — [gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/425805); [Oricon](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2454444/full/); [GameBusiness.jp](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/05/26/27040.html)
- Gundam became Bandai Namco's top-earning IP in FY3/2026, ahead of Dragon Ball and One Piece. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/gundam-sales-lifts-bandai-namcos-revenue-in-fy2026); [gundamkitscollection](https://www.gundamkitscollection.com/2026/05/gundam-dominates-bandai-namcos-fy2026.html)
- FY3/2025 Gundam IP sales were ¥153.5B (+5.3%). Contributors were Gunpla, the SEED FREEDOM film (the top-grossing Gundam theatrical release) and global streaming. — [gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/405334)

### Inferences
- Gunpla-only sales are not disclosed. With Gundam IP at ¥254B and Gunpla described as the core driver, overseas Gunpla revenue is plausibly in the tens of billions of yen per year (roughly ¥50–100B+). This is an estimate, not a disclosed figure.
- Bandai is building a third capacity step (2020, 2025/26, then Osaka 2026) while demand still exceeds supply. That is strong evidence the buyer base is growing, not flat.

### Gaps
- I could not open the IR PDFs, so I have no Gunpla-only revenue, no overseas share for FY3/2026, and no Toys & Hobby regional split (Americas / Europe / Asia) for FY3/2026.

## 5. Hobbyist behavior: kits owned, backlog (積みプラ), shortages

### Takeaway
Bandai's own survey puts the average plastic-model owner at 24.4 kits. Backlog (積みプラ, "tsumi-pla") culture is widespread and recognized in Japan, with some individuals holding hundreds of unbuilt kits. Chronic shortages since 2020 push buy-when-you-see-it behavior, which inflates backlogs and restock hunting. Together these are strong behavioral signals for inventory and backlog tracking.

### Cited Findings
- 1st BANDAI SPIRITS adult survey (Feb 2024; Japan, adults): plastic-model users own **24.4 kits on average**, and figure users 24.9. — [Bandai Spirits press](https://www.bandaispirits.co.jp/press/2024/240220.php); [PDF](https://www.bandaispirits.co.jp/pdf/press_240220.pdf)
- The 3rd survey (March 2026, n=1,032, ages 18–59) was about random goods, not Gunpla backlog, so it adds nothing here. — [Bandai Spirits press 2026](https://www.bandaispirits.co.jp/press/2026/260324.php)
- A Twitter poll on "how many 積みプラ do you have" drew 893 votes; the detailed distribution is on the page. Blogs describe backlogs of 10–30 as common and some of 200–600. — [umintyu.com](https://umintyu.com/model/information/55365/3); [Yahoo知恵袋](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14319315679); [CareerConnection](https://news.careerconnection.jp/entame/129253/); [note/yokopra](https://note.com/yokopra/n/neaa6765bd582)
- Gunpla has been in short supply since spring 2020. Bandai raises production and fights scalpers. — [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/media-entertainment/gundam-rising-bandai-namco-boosts-output-of-hot-model-kits); [Power Action explainer](https://poweraction.substack.com/p/explainer-the-gundam-model-kit-boom)
- Overseas retailers run "Restocked & New Arrivals" feeds, which shows restock-hunting behavior. — [Shokunin Gunpla](https://shokuningunpla.com/blogs/restocked-new-arrivals)

### Inferences
- Owners average about 24 kits, and the shortage-driven habit is to buy on sight. That creates the exact problem a tracker solves: what do I own, what is built, what is in the backlog, and what do I want. Scarcity also makes wishlist and restock alerts valuable.

### Gaps
- There is no quantitative survey of overseas or English-speaking kit ownership or backlog size. Reddit threads on this could not be retrieved (search returned nothing relevant).
- The Japan 24.4 average may not carry over to overseas fans.
- Negative evidence to note: supply shortages cap how fast new fans can buy. Bandai's expected capacity increases for 2026 may ease scarcity, which would reduce the restock-alert value, though not the collection-tracking value.
