## Which viral/trending US apps (2024-2026) are absent or weak in Japan?

### Takeaway
Most of the famous US viral concepts already have a Japanese version or a strong Japanese incumbent. This is especially true of AI calorie photo tracking (あすけん plus several Japanese-language Cal AI clones), couple apps (Pairy, Between, SumOne), step-for-points apps (トリマ) and ADHD planners (Tiimo is localized into Japanese). The best remaining openings are niche adaptations, not direct clones: a gamified self-care "pet" (Finch-type), AI journaling/diary, AI homework help tied to Japanese curricula and exams, and an AI money coach built around 新NISA, ふるさと納税 and 年末調整. None of the Japanese "gaps" below was confirmed with store data. They are inferences from finding no incumbent.

### Inferences
- **Direct clones of Cal AI, couple apps or step-rewards will be crowded in Japan.** Incumbents and Japanese-language copycats are already there. Calsee showed that TV exposure can still push a new AI calorie app into the top 10, so it is possible, but that depends on distribution, not on a product gap.
- **Finch-type gamified self-care** is the strongest "big US revenue, unclear Japan presence" candidate. Japan's 育成/キャラクター culture (たまごっち, LINE stickers) suggests a good cultural fit. Finch's Japan status needs checking in App Store Connect or the JP store before committing.
- **Umax-type face rating** faces heavy ethical and App Store review risk (minors, body image). It would be safer adapted as a "メンズ美容/スキンケアAI coach" without numerical attractiveness scores. There is no hard Japan demand data.
- The **Tiimo-type** space has one localized leader, but Japan-specific 発達障害 (neurodivergent) framing is thin (only a few Japanese-content apps were found). A narrower niche is still possible, for example 大人の発達障害 × 家事/通勤 routines.

## Which concepts have culturally transferable demand in Japan, and which do not?

### Takeaway
Demand transfers for diet/PFC tracking, gamified self-care or character pets, diary/journaling, exam-oriented study help and money management. It does not transfer for Christian prayer apps, cash-advance finance (Cleo's model) or aggressive attractiveness scoring. Each transferable concept needs Japan-specific data or content.

### Cited Findings
- Hallow's revenue is tied to the Christian calendar: Lent spikes ($10M in March 2024) and 263K downloads on Ash Wednesday — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/hallow-lent-surge-prayer-app-revenue). This demand driver does not exist in Japan **[inference]**.
- Japanese diet demand is proven at scale: あすけん has 13M members and more than 9 billion meal records — [asken trend report PR](https://adv.tokyo-np.co.jp/prtimes/article94129/)
- Couple apps in Japan are praised as "long-term usable" and useful for long-distance relationships — [my-best](https://my-best.com/6082)
- Cleo's growth depends partly on cash advances — [glbgpt](https://www.glbgpt.com/resource/from-zero-to-280m-the-ai-money-app-revolution)

### Inferences
- **Transferable:**
  - Calorie/PFC tracking needs a Japanese food DB covering コンビニ, 定食 and chain menus.
  - A self-care pet needs Japanese character art and tone.
  - AI diary fits the 日記/手帳 culture.
  - Homework help must be aligned to 学習指導要領, 共通テスト and 英検.
  - A money coach would cover 新NISA, iDeCo, ふるさと納税 and 年末調整/確定申告 guidance.
- **Not transferable:** Christian prayer and Bible apps. A possible substitute is 写経/御朱印/mindfulness, but that is speculative and has no data. Payday-style cash advances are ruled out by lending regulation (see Q4). Face rating aimed at teens is also out.
- **Transferability unclear:** Rosebud-style AI journaling is small even in the US (7,500 payers), so the ceiling may be low.

### Gaps
- No Japanese survey data on willingness to pay for self-care pet or journaling apps.
- No Japan search-volume data (Google Trends or ASO keyword volume) was retrieved.

## What are the Japan-specific regulatory or data hurdles?

### Takeaway
Health apps must stay on the "general wellness information" side of the MHLW guidelines on when software counts as a medical device (プログラム医療機器該当性ガイドライン), or they risk becoming medical-device software (SaMD). Finance apps must avoid lending and investment advice. Point or reward apps raise premium and prepaid-value issues. Data hurdles are a Japanese food nutrition DB and Japanese exam content.

### Cited Findings
- MHLW's guideline (issued March 2021, revised March 2023) decides medical-device status by how much the output contributes to diagnosis or treatment and by the health risk if it malfunctions — [PMDA PDF](https://www.pmda.go.jp/files/000240233.pdf)
- Apps that only provide general information for keeping healthy or improving lifestyle, without pointing to specific disease risk, are likely not medical devices — [Ushijima & Partners](https://www.ushijima-law.gr.jp/topics/20240509healthcare/), [GVA Law](https://gvalaw.jp/blog/g20210806/)
- Nutrition-therapy apps giving guidance to patients with diabetes and other conditions fall into the medical-device evaluation zone — [search summary of MHLW guidance](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/0000179749_00004.html)

### Inferences (need verification with a lawyer)
- **Calorie / diet apps:** avoid disease claims such as 糖尿病改善. 薬機法 advertising rules and 景品表示法 also apply to marketing copy. The main data hurdle is a Japanese food DB. 日本食品標準成分表 (MEXT) is public, but chain and コンビニ menus are not **[unverified]**.
- **Finance apps:** Cleo-style cash advances would fall under 貸金業法, and personalized investment advice may require 投資助言業 registration under 金商法. Bank aggregation requires 電子決済等代行業 registration **[unverified; not researched]**.
- **Step / point rewards:** 資金決済法 (prepaid instruments) and 景品表示法 premium limits may apply **[unverified]**.
- **Face rating / minors:** App Store guidelines and 個人情報保護法 handling of facial images (biometric data) apply. Target 18+ only.
- **Education:** copyright on past exam questions (過去問), and accuracy liability.
