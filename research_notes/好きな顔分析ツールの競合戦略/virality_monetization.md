# Demand, Virality and Monetization of 診断 (Diagnostic) Tools — for a "Favorite Face Type Analysis" Tool

Research date: 2026-09-23. Method note: Almost every full-page fetch was blocked by the network egress proxy (fortune.com, kaotype.jp, prtimes.jp, revenuecat.com, ja.wikipedia.org, appmarketinglabo.net). Most findings below therefore come from **search-result snippets**, not full reads of the page. Figures marked **UNVERIFIED** come from secondary or estimate sources (SEO blogs, traffic estimators, forums), or are snippet claims that could not be checked against the primary source.

## 1. Demand signals for "what's my type / 顔の好み / 顔タイプ" content

### Takeaway
There is strong, long-lasting demand for personality and face-type self-classification among Japanese and Korean Gen Z women, and "AI face diagnosis" (顔面偏差値, celebrity lookalike) is a crowded, SNS-driven app category. I found **no direct metric** for "好きな顔" (the preference side, meaning which faces you like rather than which face you have). That niche looks largely untested or unmeasured, which could mean white space or could mean low latent demand.

### Cited Findings
- MBTI diagnosis ranked #10 in the LINE Research youth trend survey for H1 2024, after building since the previous year. — [LINEヤフー LINEリサーチ 2024上半期](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/ja/news/release/008929/) (via search snippet)
- MERY Z世代研究所 survey (Sept 2024): 41.3% of Gen Z women had taken the MBTI test, about double the rate for women in their 30s and 40s. More than 60% of Gen Z respondents knew the MBTI types of the people around them. — [PR TIMES / MERY](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000118.000029212.html) (snippet; full release blocked)
- About 54% of Gen Z said MBTI was "trending" as of 2025, which makes it a long-running trend rather than a short fad. — [CANVAS ゼットモ / fun-japan](https://canvas.d2cr.co.jp/ztomo-mbti/) (snippet; source attribution within results is ambiguous — UNVERIFIED)
- Japanese media coverage says MBTI is "replacing blood-type diagnosis" through its あるある (relatability) feel. Gen Z also uses it for self-PR in job hunting. — [Diamond Online](https://diamond.jp/articles/-/338754); [マネーポストWEB](https://www.moneypost.jp/1047658)
- Korea: a Hankook Research survey found more than half of Koreans had taken the MBTI, and 9 in 10 of those aged 19–28 had. — [Korea Times, 2023](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/nation/2023/09/113_335268.html)

The content centers on 16-type face classification, comparison with K-pop idols, and matching makeup. — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/%E9%A1%94%E3%82%BF%E3%82%A4%E3%83%9716%E7%A8%AE%E9%A1%9E%E5%88%86%E3%81%91%E3%81%A6%E3%81%BF%E3%81%9F?lang=ja-JP) — UNVERIFIED figure
- Adjacent TikTok discover pages exist for 顔面偏差値 診断エフェクト, 国別顔診断, 何顔診断 男子 and 美人が多い顔タイプ, which shows many sub-genres of face diagnosis. — [TikTok discover 顔面偏差値](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/%E9%A1%94%E9%9D%A2%E5%81%8F%E5%B7%AE%E5%80%A4-%E8%A8%BA%E6%96%AD%E3%82%A8%E3%83%95%E3%82%A7%E3%82%AF%E3%83%88-%E3%82%84%E3%82%8A%E6%96%B9)

- A celebrity-lookalike and face-balance AI app featured on TV has passed 10M downloads. The app was not named in the snippet. — [search snippet via vidnoz / ai-kenkyujo](https://jp.vidnoz.com/ai-solutions/ai-face-diagnosis.html) — UNVERIFIED
- Japanese comparison media (マイベスト, Good!Apps, AppMatch) run monthly-updated "顔診断アプリ ranking" pages as of July–Aug 2026. That signals ongoing search demand and affiliate or SEO competition. — [マイベスト](https://my-best.com/6442); [AppMatch](https://appmatch.jp/178798695284911398-2)

### Inferences
- Demand for "classify me into a type" content is proven in Japan and Korea (MBTI, 顔タイプ). A tool that types **your preference** ("your favorite-face type is X") can borrow MBTI's social currency: knowing friends' types, talking about compatibility.
- The face-scoring space (顔面偏差値) is saturated and ethically fraught (see section 3). A "what faces you like" framing avoids rating the user's own looks, which is a positioning difference.

## 2. How diagnostic tools go viral (mechanics, with numbers)

### Takeaway
Virality comes from (a) an identity label short enough to put in a bio, (b) a visual result card built for Stories/X, and (c) a social comparison hook: knowing friends' types, compatibility, "which type are you?" replies. 16Personalities and Spotify Wrapped show the scale when results are a shareable identity artifact. Wrapped 2025 had 500M+ shares.

### Cited Findings
- 16Personalities (founded 2011, Lithuanian team, NERIS Analytics Ltd): the NERIS Type Explorer has reportedly been completed 1.5B+ times in 49 languages, by a team of fewer than 20 people. — [JobCannon industry report 2026](https://jobcannon.io/blog/personality-test-industry-report-2026) — UNVERIFIED (secondary blog)
- 16personalities.com traffic: about 22.3M monthly visits (Similarweb); 17.9M–20.75M/month (Semrush, late 2025); about 23M monthly visitors (hypestat). Organic search brings about 55% of desktop visits and direct about 38%. — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/16personalities.com/); [Semrush](https://cw.semrush.com/website/16personalities.com/overview); [hypestat](https://hypestat.com/info/16personalities.com) — estimates
- Inpages estimates 16Personalities' organic traffic value at $7.02M/month. — [Inpages](https://inpages.ai/insight/marketing-strategy/16personalities.com) — UNVERIFIED estimate
- Spotify Wrapped 2025: 200M+ engaged users in the first 24h (+19% YoY; 2024 took 62h to reach 200M), 250M engagements in under 3 days, and **500M+ shares (+41% YoY)**. Japan was one of the markets driving the metrics. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/04/spotify-says-wrapped-2025-is-its-biggest-yet-with-200m-users-in-its-first-day); [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/music/news/spotify-wrapped-breaks-own-record-250-million-engagements-1236603493/); [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-wrapped-campaign-hit-200m-engaged-users-in-24-hours-a-19-yoy-increase/)
- Spotify credited Wrapped with helping reach a record 751M MAU in Q4 2025. — [Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/2026/2/spotify-wrapped-drives-record-751-million-monthly-users)
- Umax (AI face rating) reportedly got 1B+ impressions across social platforms, largely because users filmed screenshots of the app as a backdrop in their own looksmaxxing videos. That is a UGC loop, not paid ads. — [Yahoo Finance / Fortune](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-163942148.html) (snippet)
- 診断メーカー (ShindanMaker) is a UGC platform where anyone can create random 診断 quizzes that post straight to Twitter/X. Its virality comes from X-native share text plus name input. — [Wikipedia JA (snippet)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%A8%BA%E6%96%AD%E3%83%A1%E3%83%BC%E3%82%AB%E3%83%BC); [shindanmaker.com/create](https://shindanmaker.com/create)
- Among quiz types, personality quizzes are the most shared, and personalized results beat generic ones on shareability. — [bestcolorfulsocks blog](https://bestcolorfulsocks.com/blogs/news/quiz-result-sharing-rate-trends) — UNVERIFIED (low-quality source, no methodology)
- MERY's finding that more than 60% of Gen Z know friends' MBTI types shows the type label spreads as social vocabulary. — [PR TIMES / MERY](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000118.000029212.html) (snippet)

### Inferences
- Design implications: give each result a named type (for example a 16-type naming system) plus a vertical 9:16 result card with a type name, an illustration or collage of the chosen faces, and a rarity % ("only 4% have this type"). Add a compare-with-friends link. The result should work as a TikTok/Reels backdrop, like the Umax screenshot loop.
- A named taxonomy that becomes shared vocabulary (like MBTI or 顔タイプ's "フレッシュ/キュート…") is the durable virality engine, more than any single shareable image.

- No verified K/share-rate coefficients for quiz sites.

## 3. Monetization models with revenue evidence

### Takeaway
Revenue evidence exists for four models. (a) Freemium premium report (16Personalities, $32.99; revenue only estimated). (b) Weekly-subscription AI face apps (Umax, about $500K/month, $3.99/week). (c) Offline certification and B2B (日本顔タイプ診断協会: about ¥198K per adviser course, 5,000+ certified). (d) Display ads on quiz sites, which have low RPMs (about $0.8–3 in one case study). Affiliate is plausible but I found no hard revenue numbers for it.

### Cited Findings
**Premium reports (16Personalities)**
- Premium profile at $32.99/year, with an estimated conversion rate under 2%. Estimated total revenue of $15–25M/year from ads plus premium. — [JobCannon industry report 2026](https://jobcannon.io/blog/personality-test-industry-report-2026) — UNVERIFIED (estimate; NERIS doesn't publish financials)
- A 2026 review discusses 16Personalities Premium cost and accuracy criticism (the "MBTI problem"). — [MyPassion.AI review](https://mypassion.ai/blog/16personalities-career-test-review)

**Weekly subscription AI face apps (Umax-style)**
- Umax: $3.99/week. The face scan is free and the result is paywalled ("the scan is free, the result costs $3.99/week"). — [Real World Appeal](https://realworldappeal.com/en/blog/umax-ai); [Yahoo/Fortune snippet](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-163942148.html)
- Umax: 7M+ downloads, peaked at #36 in US App Store Lifestyle, about $500K/month in subscription revenue, and 90% of users are men aged 16–45 (Fortune, July 2024). — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/07/01/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-faces-mental-health/) (fetch blocked; figures from search summary — UNVERIFIED in detail). A BlackHatWorld thread also claims $500K MRR — [BlackHatWorld](https://www.blackhatworld.com/seo/looksmaxxing-app-exploiting-mens-beauty-makes-500k-mrr.1599755/) (forum)

- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026: weekly plans now generate **55.5% of subscription revenue** for most categories. Median first renewal for weekly plans is 35–58%, and **Social & Lifestyle is lowest at 35%**. Renewal rates jump about 25–30pp between the first and second renewal. Trial conversion medians are 9.1% for Business and 4.4% for Gaming. There are 5,000+ certified advisers, and a network of partner salons/academies (e.g., HAPPY SPIRAL Academy in Ginza, イメージコンサルティングサロンMore) resell the course. — [kaotype.jp course page](https://kaotype.jp/course/kaotype-adviser1/); [HAPPY SPIRAL Academy](https://happyspiral-academy.com/kaotype-adviser1/); [imagesalon-more](https://imagesalon-more.com/service/face-type-advisor-1st-grade-certification/); [beaute-p](https://www.beaute-p.com/knowledge/reg-4/)
- Implied gross (inference only, not reported): 5,000 × ¥198K ≈ ¥1B cumulative course revenue if every adviser paid the full 1級 price. — UNVERIFIED arithmetic; the real mix of grades and discounts is unknown.

**Display ads on quiz sites**
- Case study: the dating/relationship quiz and entertainment site A New Mode raised its RPM from $0.80 to an all-time high of $3 after adding DFP ad units and Google AdX with dynamic allocation. — [Adnimation case study](https://www.adnimation.com/new-mode-monetization-case-study/)
- Viral quiz sites (targeting millennial women with "which character are you?" quizzes) historically ran click arbitrage: buying cheap Facebook newsfeed traffic and monetizing it with display ads. — [Adbeat](https://blog.adbeat.com/the-business-of-display-part-2-viral-quiz-sites-and-click-arbitrage/)
- A Japanese diagnostic-app business ("診断アプリで売上計画5億円", "アクセスを流して収益化") monetized traffic by sending it to other apps and services. — [アプリマーケティング研究所](https://appmarketinglabo.net/shindanapp-business/) (title and snippet only; fetch blocked — details UNVERIFIED)

**Affiliate**
- Personality assessments are commonly monetized by tying results to relevant affiliate offers. — [Indie Hackers snippet](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/4-years-26-projects-115k-lessons-from-an-indie-hacker-7ab46733da) (generic claim)
- Japanese comparison media (マイベスト, AppMatch, Good!Apps) rank 顔診断 apps, which suggests an affiliate/SEO ecosystem around the category. — [マイベスト](https://my-best.com/6442)

### Inferences
- For a "favorite face type" tool, the models that best fit the product and the evidence:
  1. A free result card with a paid detailed report, either a one-time unlock (¥300–¥980 range is typical for JP 診断; this price is **my assumption, not sourced**) or a 16P-style premium.
  2. Contextual affiliate: dating apps (the "your type" framing leads naturally to matchmaking), makeup and hair tied to 顔タイプ, and K-pop and idol content.
  3. Display ads as a baseline only. At about $1–3 RPM, ads need millions of pageviews to matter.
- Weekly subscriptions monetize strongly (Umax at about $500K/month), but they depend on repeat "score improvement" loops and scanning the user's own face. Social & Lifestyle has the weakest weekly renewal (35%). A preference-analysis tool has less natural weekly repetition, so a weekly subscription is a poorer fit unless repeat features exist (see section 4).
- A B2B certification model like 顔タイプ's requires owning a trademarked taxonomy plus a human-trainer network. It is high margin but slow to build.

### Gaps
- No verified 16Personalities revenue: NERIS is private, and the $15–25M figure is a third-party estimate.
- No Japan-specific RPM data for 診断 sites.
- No hard conversion data on dating-app or cosmetics affiliate from quiz traffic.
- Glowy-type app revenue wasn't found.

## 4. Retention of one-shot diagnostics and tactics for repeat usage

### Takeaway
One-shot diagnostics have inherently low retention. No hard benchmark was found, but the evidence points indirectly the same way: 16Personalities depends on organic search (new users), and Social & Lifestyle weekly subscriptions renew at only 35%. Repeat use comes from turning the result into a social object (friends' types, compatibility) or a progress loop (Umax weekly re-scoring, Wrapped's annual ritual).

### Cited Findings
- About 55% of 16Personalities desktop visits come from organic search (new-intent queries) and about 38% are direct. — [Similarweb snippet](https://www.similarweb.com/website/16personalities.com/)
- Social & Lifestyle has the lowest median first renewal for weekly plans at 35%. Education has the highest at 58%. — [RevenueCat renewal benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/average-subscription-renewal-rates-by-app-category)
- Umax drives repeat use by re-scoring the user's face weekly (score up to 100) with improvement tips such as starting a skincare routine. — [Yahoo/Fortune snippet](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-163942148.html)
- Spotify Wrapped is an annual ritual that brings users back. It hit 200M users in 24h, versus 62h the year before. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/04/spotify-says-wrapped-2025-is-its-biggest-yet-with-200m-users-in-its-first-day)
- Knowing friends' types (more than 60% of Gen Z know others' MBTI types) turns a one-time test into ongoing social use. — [PR TIMES / MERY](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000118.000029212.html)
- 16Personalities monetizes beyond the single test through relationship and career content (its site title is "type descriptions, relationship and career advice"). — [hypestat page title](https://hypestat.com/info/16personalities.com)

### Inferences
- Repeat-usage tactics fitting a favorite-face tool:
  - **Compare with friends**: an invite link that shows "your type vs. friend's type" overlap. Every comparison needs a second user, which also drives acquisition.
  - **Couple compatibility**: "does my partner match my favorite type?" This is highly shareable and a natural premium upsell.
  - **Evolving profile**: the type updates as the user adds or swipes more faces, like Umax's weekly re-score but without judging the user's own face.
  - **Seasonal re-test or "Wrapped" recap**: for example, "your 2026 face-preference Wrapped" built from the faces saved over the year.
  - **Celebrity and idol tie-ins**: "idols who match your type," refreshed with new idols and content.

### Gaps
- No published retention curve (D1/D7/D30) found for web 診断 tools or 16Personalities.
- No data on the lift from compatibility or "compare with friends" features.
